We are building a webapp that lets you split bills, receipts, etc. easily with your friends live and quickly. The key problem solved is splitting a bill while you are still sitting at the table, instead of tracking down all of your friends after the fact.
1. Payer flow (person who paid the bill)
Open website - Click "Start a New Split" - Enter name - Enter Venmo username and/or Zelle phone/email - No account creation required (for now - we will add logins later)
Upload receipt - Scan with camera, upload photo, or manually enter bill - AI extracts individual items, prices, subtotal, tax, and tip - Payer reviews and corrects any errors - App verifies that all amounts reconcile with the bill total
Pre / post-tip
Choose split type - Simple: Split the total equally among all participants, including the payer - Itemized: Each participant selects the items they want to pay for, with shared items supported
Generate and share link - Create a unique bill-sharing URL - Show a "Copy Link" button - Send link to friends via text or group chat
Also add a QR code and a share-screen for airdrop / better sharing
Waiting room / live dashboard - Display number of friends joined - Display each friend's name and status (Selecting / Finished) - Show live item selections or split totals - Also allow the payer to select items / similar flow as friends (so that they can choose what they bought) - Display unclaimed items and outstanding balances - Automatically update as friends make changes
Close out the bill - Option A: All friends press "Finish"; payer closes the bill - Option B: Payer selects "Force Finish" to close early - Payer resolves unclaimed items or incomplete allocations before closing OR decides there is something wrong with the split and re-opens everything - App locks selections and finalizes amounts owed
2. Friend flow (person paying their share)
Receive and open link - Open the unique URL shared by payer - No registration or password required
Quick onboarding - Enter name - Click "Join Bill" - Appear immediately on payer's live dashboard
Split selection
Path A: Simple split
Equal split
See the total bill
See number of participants
See estimated equal share, including tax and tip
Confirm share
Path B: Itemized split
Choose items
View all receipt items with prices
Select items to pay for
Split an item with one or more friends
See live subtotal plus allocated tax and tip
Confirm selections
Press "Finish" - Selections are saved - Payer's dashboard changes this friend to "Finished" - Friend enters waiting screen until bill closes
Payment screen - Once payer closes the bill, show final amount owed - Display payer's Venmo payment link or Zelle payment details - Provide "Pay with Venmo" and/or "Pay with Zelle" options - Payment occurs outside the app
